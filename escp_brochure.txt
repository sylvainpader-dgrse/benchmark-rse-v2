At ESCP, we believe that driving sustainable, ethical
and responsible change starts with a strong and
engaged community.
ESCP embeds sustainability at multiple levels, both at
the federal level, and locally on each campus. This
multi-layered approach ensures strong collaboration,
bringing together a diverse range of stakeholders—
from faculty representatives and student societies to
administrative teams and external partners.MadridLondon 
Paris
TurinBerlinESCP hosts 16 student societies dedicated to
sustainability, addressing environmental and social
challenges. The Student Sustainability Board holds
monthly meetings to coordinate initiatives across
campuses, while the other 12 societies contribute to
raising awareness and driving action for a more
sustainable future.We foster sustainable mindsets that end up creating
sustainable companies.
This coalition of top international business schools
works together to foster innovation and impact in
business education. By creating a shared platform for
research, events, and agenda-setting, it moves beyond
isolated efforts to develop a structured and long-term
approach to tackling global sustainability challenges.This advisory board brings together 12 leaders from
key fields-ranging from climate science and activism
to finance, civil society, and corporate industry-to drive
sustainability in business education and practice.
Through accountability and collective leadership, it
aims at accelerating the transition to a carbon-neutral
and equitable economy.7
