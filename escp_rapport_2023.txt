3 ACTIVITY REPORT 2023  I  Highlights  IThe year 2023 was a period of celebration, change and success at ESCP.
We celebrated the 50th anniversary of our European model, which is the very essence 
of ESCP today and which characterises our unique organisation: a multi-campus, 
multicultural and multidimensional organisation. Several celebrations took place on 
all our campuses, among our vibrant community of students, alumni, professors, 
employees, firms and partners. 
We also set up a new senior management team, led by Professor Léon Laulusa, 
which marks change in continuity following the laudable work of Professor Frank 
Bournois. Indeed, Professor Léon Laulusa has been a part of ESCP for over a quarter 
of a century now and in his previous positions he played a key role in implementing 
recent changes at the school.  
In 2023, we also temporarily left our historical campus on Avenue de la République 
in Paris, thanks to support from the Paris Île-de-France Chamber of Commerce and 
Industry. By 2028, major renovations will have brought us a campus at the cutting 
edge of innovation that respects the highest environmental standards. This period 
coincides with large-scale building works on all our campuses. 
Lastly, in 2023, we were especially pleased to gain the highest recognition of the 
efforts that each of us has made: these efforts were rewarded with outstanding 
positions in national and international rankings.
I would like to warmly thank all the members of our community for their remarkable 
involvement, which you can find out about in this report.Editorial
PHILIPPE HOUZÉ 
Chairman of the Board of Directors

5 ACTIVITY REPORT 2023  I  Highlights  IIntroduction
ESCP enjoyed a remarkable year in 2023: we celebrated our unique European model, 
we strengthened our range of training programmes, and we pursued our growth on 
a European and international level.

aim of having 10,000 students by 2025 as we now have 9,786  students of 135 nationalities.

and researchers on our six European campuses. And our annual budget now amounts 
to €176m.

on a path to the year 2030 and beyond. We aim for ESCP to be recognised clearly and 
lastingly as one of the world’s top 10 business schools.

FOCUS  Competency Passport 
ESCP has created a ‘Competency Passport’: a digital platform run by the Careers Department 
to improve support and professional development for students on initial degree courses. This 
innovative tool turns the skills, knowledge and experiences that a student has gained into 
professional and behavioural competencies. The data in the student’s Competency Passport can 
change and expand over the course of their higher education, all the way up to their graduation. 
The Competency Passport summarises the professional competencies that are specific to 
each programme and 25 behavioural competencies that reflect ESCP’s DNA and that relate to 
multiculturalism, entrepreneurship, digitalisation, ESG criteria and the hybridisation of areas of 
expertise. 
Integrating AI technologies to serve students 
ESCP incorporates the latest technology, including artificial intelligence, into its programmes and 
student services. In May 2023, we adopted an Artificial Intelligence User Charter. Access to AI 
tools has gradually been broadened in the school. Professors, employees and students have been

Train the trainers  Alongside creation of new lessons, reflections on existing content forms 
part of the essential projects to implement in our school. In 2023, ESCP therefore introduced a 
programme called ‘Train the Trainers’, offering professors several days of training so they could 
consider incorporating the topic of sustainability into their lessons and identify obstacles to 
doing so.  This programme was developed in partnership with ‘Le Campus de la Transition’, 
an ecology-focussed training centre in the Paris region that draws inspiration from Satish 
Kumar’s philosophy. 
Commons for the future This project is a partnership with ESCP Transition Network, a

employees. It is a website that provides – in open access – all content from the optional module 
‘Energy, Business, Climate and Geopolitics’ that is taught in the second year of the Master 
programme.
